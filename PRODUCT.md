# Product

## Register

product

## Users

AIQuizLite primarily serves individual learners and exam candidates. Teachers or content curators may publish study materials and practice banks, but the learner remains the primary user of the app interface.

## Product Purpose

Turn teacher-provided materials, industry learning resources, and selected open-source educational content into portable question banks. Help learners practise, review explanations, identify weak areas, and remember knowledge through repeated local study.

## Brand Personality

Clear, focused, trustworthy.

## Anti-references

Do not resemble a short-video feed, entertainment-first content app, noisy gamified learning product, or complex teacher administration system. Avoid interfaces that hide core study actions behind promotional content, excessive badges, or decorative motion.

## Design Principles

1. Keep the current study task visible and easy to continue.
2. Preserve the source, answer, and explanation context needed for trustworthy review.
3. Make question banks portable while keeping local data and user choices under the learner's control.
4. Add advanced capabilities progressively without crowding the primary practice flow.
5. Prefer explicit labels and reversible actions for settings, imports, corrections, and data management.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Follow normal Android usability conventions, readable contrast, touch-target sizing, and semantic labels. No additional large-text, high-contrast, color-vision, or reduced-motion modes are required in the current phase.
